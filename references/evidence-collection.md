## Purpose

This guide teaches students what to collect, how to collect it, and how to organize it so that it remains useful for platform complaints, labor inspection, legal aid, or arbitration. It covers screenshots, photos, chat exports, call recordings, and on-site documentation.

The skill does not promise that any specific piece of evidence will be admitted in court. The goal is to create a clear, contemporaneous, attributable record that supports the student's account and speeds up official investigation.

## General Principles

1. Collect before deleting, blocking, or returning anything.
2. Keep originals untouched; work from copies when annotating or redacting.
3. Record who said what, when, where, and through which channel.
4. Corroborate single-party statements with independent sources whenever possible.
5. Separate verified facts from hearsay, inferences, and recruiter claims in notes.

## Recording: When And How

### When to record
- any phone call where the other party confirms job title, wage, location, start date, fees, or housing;
- on-site conversations where the employer or intermediary explains changed terms, fees, or waiting periods;
- verbal promises that differ from written terms;
- threats, pressure tactics, or requests to keep arrangements secret.

### How to record
- Use the phone's built-in voice memo or recording app.
- Start recording before the other party begins speaking, if possible.
- State the date, time, location, and identities of the speakers at the start of the recording.
- Keep the phone stable and screen-off to avoid accidental noise or interruptions.
- Save the file immediately after the call or conversation ends.
- Do not edit, trim, or filter the recording.

### What to say to start a recording
> 现在是[日期][时间]，我在[地点]，和[对方姓名/称呼]通话/谈话。内容关于[岗位/费用/工作安排]，请确认[关键问题]。

This anchors the recording in time, place, and subject.

### Questions that should be recorded
- "请再确认一下工资结算方式，是按月5500还是按天结算？"
- "之前说的700元保险，如果干不满45天怎么处理？"
- "实际用工单位是哪家公司？签约主体是哪家？"
- "培训期和等待期有没有工资？"
- "今天安排的具体工作内容是什么？地点在哪里？"
- "如果我现在要离开，费用怎么退？工资怎么结算？"

These questions draw out admissions that can later be compared with written terms.

## Recording Content Checklist

A useful recording should capture:
- [ ] date and time of the conversation
- [ ] exact location
- [ ] full names or identifying descriptions of all speakers
- [ ] the specific job title, site, and dates discussed
- [ ] wage amount, basis, and payment date
- [ ] any fee, deposit, insurance, or penalty mentioned
- [ ] any change to previously agreed terms
- [ ] any threat, pressure, or instruction to keep silent
- [ ] the other party's admission of who employs, manages, or pays the student

If the other party refuses to answer a key question, record that refusal verbatim.

## Screenshot And Photo Standards

### What to capture
- recruitment listing: full page URL, title, wage, location, publisher name, publication date, and any "chat to apply" button or contact method;
- chat messages: complete conversation, not cropped selections; include dates, names, and profile pictures if they identify the speaker;
- payment records: amount, payee name, account, date, and purpose; screenshot both the request and the successful transfer;
- company signage: full company name, license, or badge on the door, wall rules, attendance machines, and receipts;
- receipts and invoices: item, amount, issuer, date, and whether a refund term is stated;
- documents: every page of a contract, offer, fee schedule, or policy; include handwritten notes, blank fields, and attachments.

### How to capture
- use the phone's built-in screenshot or screen recording;
- for web listings, capture the URL bar and scroll to show the full context;
- for physical documents, photograph in good light with all text legible;
- include a contemporaneous note of the date, time, and location of the photo;
- do not edit, crop, or annotate the original file; keep a clean copy and make annotations on a separate copy if needed.

### Naming convention
Use a consistent format: `YYYYMMDD_HHMM_type_description.extension`

Examples:
- `20260724_1430_listing_security_guard_beijing.jpg`
- `20260724_1500_chat_recruiter_wechat.png`
- `20260724_1600_receipt_medical_exam_328yuan.jpg`
- `20260724_1700_contract_page1_signed.jpg`

This keeps evidence chronological and searchable.

## Chat And Call Records

### Exporting chat records
- WeChat: select the contact, open chat settings, export chat history or use the built-in backup to computer;
- QQ: similar export function in chat settings;
- other platforms: use the platform's official export or backup feature;
- do not rely on scrolling screenshots alone; export the full record if the platform allows it.

### What to include in chat exports
- full contact names or account IDs;
- group chat context if relevant;
- dates and times;
- any voice message that should be transcribed;
- any file, image, or link shared.

### Transcribing voice messages
- Record the voice message playback through the phone speaker into a voice memo, or use the platform's built-in transcription if available.
- Note the original voice message time, sender, and duration.
- Keep the original voice message in the chat; do not delete it.

### Call records
- Phone calls: check the phone's call log for number, date, duration, and contact name.
- VoIP calls (WeChat, QQ, etc.): note the platform, contact, date, and approximate duration.
- If the call content is important, record it with the guidelines above.

## Payment Evidence

For every payment, collect:
- the payment request or invoice (screenshot or photo);
- the successful transfer record (screenshot from the bank app, payment platform, or chat transfer function);
- the payee's exact name, account number or payment ID, and the stated purpose;
- whether a receipt, invoice, or refund promise was given;
- the relationship between the payee and the supposed employer.

If the payee is a personal account, record that fact explicitly. Personal payment accounts for employment-related fees are a risk signal.

## Contract And Document Evidence

- photograph or scan every page, including blank pages, handwritten changes, and attachments;
- record the date of signing, the full legal names of all parties, and the seal or signature placement;
- if the other party retains the original, request a complete copy on the spot and photograph the copy they hand over;
- note any missing page, blank field, or reference to external rules that were not provided.

## On-Site Evidence

When arriving at the work site:
- photograph the building exterior, company name on the door, and nearby street signs;
- photograph any posted rules, schedules, forms, badges, or receipts;
- note whether the company name on the door matches the name on the contract, listing, and seal;
- note whether licenses, permits, or employee identification can be produced;
- record the names and roles of people who give instructions or show you around;
- if many other applicants are present, note whether they have specific roles, schedules, or work sites.

Do not photograph other people's identity documents or private areas without consent.

## Evidence Organization And Naming

Create a master folder named with the case date and a short identifier, such as `20260724_security_guard_beijing`.

Inside, create subfolders:
- `01_listing`: recruitment listings, screenshots, URLs;
- `02_chat`: chat exports, screenshots, voice transcriptions;
- `03_payment`: receipts, invoices, transfer records;
- `04_contract`: contracts, offers, fee schedules, policies;
- `05_onsite`: photos of the site, signage, badges, schedules;
- `06_calls`: call logs, recordings, voice notes;
- `07_correspondence`: sent emails, messages, complaint drafts;
- `08_timeline`: the master timeline document.

Within each subfolder, use the `YYYYMMDD_HHMM_type_description.extension` naming convention.

Keep a plain-text timeline file in `08_timeline` that lists events in chronological order with file references. Update it as new evidence is collected.

## Privacy And Redaction Rules

When sharing evidence with platforms, legal aid, or labor inspection:
- redact the student's full identity number, bank account number, precise home address, signature, and face before photographing or sharing;
- redact the same information for any other student whose documents appear in the evidence;
- keep the redacted version for sharing and the original for official procedures if required;
- do not publish other students' identity information or make public accusations while facts remain unresolved.

## Legal Admissibility Notes

- A recording made by a participant in the conversation is generally treated as a legitimate evidence acquisition method in civil disputes in mainland China, provided it was not obtained by illegal means such as installing a listening device in someone else's private space or using胁迫手段.
- The probative value depends on completeness, authenticity, and correlation with other evidence.
- A recording that has been edited, trimmed, or filtered is likely to be excluded or given little weight.
- The skill should not promise that a specific recording will be admitted; instead, it should maximize the student's ability to produce a complete, authentic, and relevant record.
- When in doubt, advise the student to consult current local legal-aid channels for the specific jurisdiction's practice.
