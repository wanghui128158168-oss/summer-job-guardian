# Official Research Starting Points

Use these as starting points, not as proof of a particular job. Verify that the page is current, official, and applicable to the user's location and date.

## National Starting Points

- National Laws and Regulations Database: <https://flk.npc.gov.cn/>
- State Council policy and public-service portal: <https://www.gov.cn/>
- Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security: <https://www.mohrss.gov.cn/>
- National Human Resources and Social Security Government Service Platform: <https://www.12333.gov.cn/>
- National Enterprise Credit Information Publicity System: <https://www.gsxt.gov.cn/>
- China Legal Service Network / public legal-service starting point: <https://www.12348.gov.cn/>

Do not rely on a search-result snippet. Open the source and record title, issuing authority, effective status, date, jurisdiction, and relevant text.

Some official systems use interactive verification, geographic routing, or anti-automation controls. A `403`, `521`, challenge page, or failed automated request does not prove that the authority or record is invalid. Try the official page in a normal browser, use the relevant local authority's public page or phone, and record the access limitation instead of substituting an unofficial database without disclosure.

## Legal And Regulatory Topics To Retrieve

Search current official versions rather than storing remembered article numbers:

- `中华人民共和国劳动合同法` for document retention, guarantees, property collection, written terms, wages, and dispatch relationships;
- `人力资源市场暂行条例` for human-resources-service activities, licensing, disclosed information, and supervision;
- `网络招聘服务管理规定` for online recruitment-service duties and recruitment-information requirements;
- `就业服务与就业管理规定` for recruitment and employment-service conduct;
- `劳动保障监察条例` for labor-inspection scope and procedure;
- current local minimum-wage, wage-payment, internship, part-time, minor-worker, and public-employment rules;
- role-specific rules for security, screening, food, events, occupational health, or certificates.

## Local Verification

Find the official city or province pages for:

- human-resources-service and labor-dispatch license or permit disclosures;
- labor inspection, wage complaint, public employment, and `12333` services;
- `12345`, `12348`, police, emergency, and legal-aid service scope;
- minimum wage and wage-payment rules;
- short-term rental, housing registration, and subletting rules where relevant;
- job-specific training, certificate issuer, fees, and validity.

Record the exact agency and geographic jurisdiction. A hotline number can be a starting point, but the agent should verify the current service and avoid promising acceptance.

## Source Integrity Checks

- Confirm official domain and issuing body.
- Check whether the text is effective, amended, repealed, or only a draft or interpretation.
- Distinguish national law, local rule, agency guidance, platform policy, and media explanation.
- Quote only the portion that supports the conclusion.
- Explain what remains fact-specific.
