## Safety First

If the user reports threat, confinement, injury, document confiscation, unexpected relocation, or fear of leaving:

1. Keep the first response short.
2. Encourage moving to a public safe place when feasible.
3. Contact a trusted person and share current location and party details.
4. Use current local emergency, police, or medical channels as appropriate.
5. Do not encourage confrontation, secret investigation, or recovery of money before safety.

Verify local numbers when outside mainland China. In mainland China, common starting points include police `110` and medical emergency `120`; current local availability and alternatives should still be checked when practical.

## On-Site Trapping And Bait-And-Switch

When the student has already arrived at the work site and discovers that conditions differ materially from the advertisement or contract, apply the on-site emergency workflow in `references/on-site-emergency.md` before ordinary analysis.

Key points for this scenario:
- Separate safety from economics. A safe but deceptive arrangement still requires a clear decision.
- Do not let sunk costs (travel, fees, deposits, time already spent) distort the decision. Calculate the expected net outcome of staying versus leaving, including continuing living costs and opportunity cost.
- If the employer uses reduced work assignments, unpaid waiting, or new fees to pressure the student into staying or recruiting others, treat this as a hard stop.
- Preserve evidence on site before departing: company name on the door, rules, schedules, badges, receipts, and a contemporaneous note of what was said and by whom.
- Do not sign any retroactive document, settlement, or acknowledgment without reading and understanding every term.

The decision options remain `stop now`, `pause and verify`, or `continue cautiously`, but the on-site framework adds a structured stay-or-leave calculation and evidence-first departure protocol.

## Stop Further Loss

Recommend, as applicable:

- stop new payments and do not borrow to recover an earlier payment;
- do not recruit classmates to unlock a refund or commission;
- stop sending identity images, bank data, verification codes, or facial-recognition material;
- contact the bank or payment platform through official channels if account compromise is possible;
- change exposed credentials from a safe device and review connected sessions;
- preserve evidence before blocking accounts, deleting chats, or returning devices;
- avoid signing retroactive or blank documents to obtain wages or leave.

## Evidence Bundle

Preserve lawfully obtained originals and exports:

- original listing URL, screenshots, account identity, and publication time;
- complete chat export with dates, names, and context;
- phone numbers, call records, and contemporaneous notes;
- payment record, payee name, account, receipt, invoice, and refund promise;
- contract, offer, form, policy, fee schedule, certificate notice, and each version;
- attendance, schedule, location, work product, uniform, badge, supervisor instruction, and witness contacts;
- company signs, office address, work site, transport, and documents showing party names;
- wage calculation, promised pay date, partial payment, deductions, and requests for payment;
- incident timeline and any official case or complaint reference number.

Keep unedited originals. Work from copies when annotating or redacting. Do not publish other students' identity information or make public accusations while facts remain unresolved.

## Event Timeline And Amount

Create a table:

| Date/time | Place/channel | Person/entity | Promise or event | Money/hours | Evidence file | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---:|---|---|

Calculate separately:

- fees and deposits paid;
- wages claimed, basis, workdays/hours, and pay date;
- housing, transport, medical, training, and certificate losses;
- unauthorized transactions or account exposure;
- amounts already refunded or paid.

Do not combine every inconvenience into a legally recoverable amount. Label direct loss, wage claim, incidental cost, and unknown remedy separately.

## Route By Problem

Research current local routes before recommending a filing. Starting categories may include:

- immediate threat, confinement, document theft, suspected fraud, or identity/account misuse: police and relevant financial or platform security channels;
- employer or human-resources-service conduct, wage payment, labor inspection, or public employment: local human-resources and social-security authorities or `12333` starting points;
- cross-department local coordination: `12345` where available;
- legal classification, agreement dispute, or procedure choice: current public legal-service or legal-aid channels such as `12348` where available;
- school-organized internship: school coordinator and responsible department, without relying on the school as the only route;
- platform listing or payment: official platform complaint and preservation procedures, in addition to any public route.

Verify jurisdiction, required evidence, deadline, and whether the issue is treated as labor, civil, administrative, or criminal. Do not promise that one channel will accept or recover the claim.

## Communication Templates

### Written Request For Clarification Or Refund

> 我于[日期]基于贵方关于[岗位、地点、入职日期]的说明支付/提交了[款项或材料]。目前新增或变化的条件包括[逐项列明]，与此前书面说明不一致。请贵方于[合理日期]前以公司名义说明签约主体、实际用工单位、岗位状态、费用依据及处理方案，并按原约定退还[金额]/返还[材料]。后续请通过本渠道书面回复。

### Wage Reconciliation Request

> 我于[日期]至[日期]在[地点]按[负责人]安排完成[工作]，现按已确认的[时薪/日薪/月薪/计件]核算应付[金额]，约定发薪日为[日期]。附件为考勤、工作安排及此前工资约定。请贵方书面确认工资支付主体、核算明细及具体支付日期。

### On-Site Clarification Request (before departure)

> 我于[日期]到达[地点]。此前约定的[岗位/工资/食宿/发薪日]与目前实际安排存在以下差异：[逐项列明]。请贵方于[日期]前以公司名义书面确认：签约主体、实际用工单位、具体岗位、起止日期、工资计算方式、已收费用的性质和退款安排。若无法按原约定安排，请书面说明退费或安置方案。

### On-Site Fee Refund Request

> 我于[日期]支付了[金额]用于[保险/体检/培训/押金]，当时说明[退款条件]。目前[岗位/工作安排/发薪]与约定不符。请贵方于[日期]前退还上述费用并书面说明依据。若逾期未回复，我将向[12333/12345/12348]及平台反映并保存证据。

Keep language factual and avoid threats or unsupported criminal labels.
