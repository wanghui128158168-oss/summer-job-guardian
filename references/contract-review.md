## Review Method

Ask for the complete document, including title, first page, signatures, seals, attachments, handwritten changes, and referenced policies. Ask the user to redact identity numbers, bank details, precise home address, signatures, faces, and unrelated personal information.

Quote the exact clause before explaining it. Separate:

- what the text says;
- practical risk;
- what is missing or ambiguous;
- current legal point that needs official verification;
- proposed written clarification or revision.

Do not say “the contract is safe” after finding no obvious issue. A genuine-looking contract does not verify role authorization or future performance.

## Identity And Document Integrity

Check:

- exact legal name, unified social credit code, address, representative, and contact;
- consistency among recruiter, contract party, seal, actual work user, and wage payer;
- agreement type and the relationship it claims to create;
- page numbers, cross-page seal when applicable, attachments, blank fields, corrections, and missing pages;
- signature and seal placement, date, and whether the student receives an identical complete copy;
- references to separate rules, handbooks, dormitory rules, fee schedules, or training agreements.

Do not advise signing a blank, incomplete, backdated, or unexplained document. Do not let the other party retain all copies.

## Material Terms

Review at minimum:

- role, duties, work site, transfer radius, and reporting manager;
- start/end dates, probation or trial, training, standby, and certificate period;
- daily and weekly hours, rest, shifts, overtime, attendance, and leave;
- wage amount, gross/net basis, piece-rate rules, minimum guaranteed work, pay date, method, and wage payer;
- every deduction, deposit, fee, penalty, housing cost, meal cost, equipment cost, and refund condition;
- food, accommodation, utilities, room rules, transport, and what happens after resignation;
- medical exam, insurance, injury handling, equipment, and safety training;
- termination, resignation notice, role substitution, relocation, no-work situation, and final wage payment;
- intellectual property, image use, confidentiality, personal data, and account access;
- dispute route, governing location, notices, and contact address.

Compare these terms with the advertisement and chat. A materially worse contract term should not be normalized as “standard.”

## High-Risk Clauses

Flag for clarification or rejection:

- employer may change role, city, wage, or hours at sole discretion;
- unpaid trial, training, certificate, waiting, or standby with no limit;
- wages depend entirely on undefined assessment or customer payment;
- broad authorization to deduct any expense or fine;
- resignation penalty unrelated to a clearly explained lawful basis;
- student assumes all injury, housing, property, customer, or third-party liability;
- employer may retain documents, devices, wages, deposits, or personal data;
- automatic consent to loans, accounts, commercial posting, portrait use, or personal-data sharing;
- oral promises override nothing while key promises remain outside the contract;
- blank attachments, “company rules” not supplied, or conflicting versions;
- dispute venue with no clear connection to the actual work.

Do not declare a clause legally invalid from memory. Explain the risk and verify current law or obtain local legal support when validity matters.

## Student-Work Relationship Uncertainty

Do not use student status alone to decide rights. Distinguish:

- school-organized teaching internship;
- self-found temporary work under daily management;
- labor dispatch;
- part-time arrangement;
- service or task contract;
- platform gig or independent service.

Record actual control, attendance, tools, workplace, wage method, duration, and party relationships. If the classification changes the remedy, state that it requires current local legal verification.

## Review Output

Return a clause table:

| Clause | Exact text | Status | Practical risk | Missing evidence | Proposed wording/action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

Then state:

- identity mismatches;
- top three decision-relevant issues;
- promises missing from the document;
- questions that must be answered in writing;
- decision: stop now, pause and verify, or continue cautiously for the next step;
- files and communications to preserve.

## Clarification Template

> 为避免双方理解不一致，请贵方以公司名义书面确认：签约主体、实际工作单位、工作地点、岗位、起止日期、工时与休息、工资计算方式、发薪日期、培训/考证是否计薪、全部收费和扣款、食宿条件、调岗或无岗位时的处理，以及离职后的工资结算。若与合同不一致，请在签署前修改合同并向我提供完整副本。
