## Intake

Collect only inputs that change the recommendation:

- city and acceptable commute radius;
- available dates and minimum uninterrupted duration;
- age if under 18 is possible;
- cash available before first payday and money reserved for returning home;
- whether housing is already available;
- skills, devices, portfolio, certificates, and physical constraints;
- minimum acceptable net income and whether income stability matters more than upside;
- career objective, preferred work environment, night-work limit, and safety constraints.

Do not assume every student should pursue the highest nominal wage. Identify whether the goal is career experience, reliable cash, exploration, flexibility, or a combination.

## Strategy Comparison

Compare at least two feasible models, not arbitrary categories.

### Online Internship Or Skill Work

Prefer verified company or institution channels and work that uses a real skill. Check for unpaid trial work, speculative assignments, fake data-entry work, deposits, account lending, and requests to move money. Confirm deliverables, review criteria, ownership, pay basis, payment date, supervision, and whether work can be used in a portfolio.

### Direct-Hire Local Work

Contact the actual store, venue, restaurant, retailer, event organizer, or company through an independently obtained phone number. Direct contact reduces layers but does not prove the role or terms. Confirm whether the specific location hires temporary students and who signs and pays.

### Package Room And Board

Treat food and housing as priced conditions, not free benefits. Ask:

- address, room occupancy, utilities, deposits, curfew, and photos or viewing arrangements;
- whether housing is conditional on staying in the job;
- what happens to housing and wages after resignation or reassignment;
- whether food days, portions, or deductions differ on rest days;
- whether leaving the accommodation creates a penalty.

Model the risk of losing job and housing at the same time.

### Self-Arranged Housing Plus Stable Work

This model expands the job radius and separates housing from the employer. Research real short-term availability, not just advertised monthly price. Include deposit, agency or platform fee, utilities, bedding, transport, minimum lease, subletting permission, early-exit loss, and personal-safety conditions.

### Low-Cost Housing Plus Flexible Gigs

Use only when the student has adequate cash runway, a safe housing plan, multiple verified sources of gigs, and independent return-home money. Keep one baseline income source when possible and add flexible work around it.

Compare:

- expected, downside, and break-even income;
- number of bookable days rather than advertised opportunities;
- cancellation and no-show rules;
- commute time and late-night return safety;
- payment delay and evidence available;
- injury, equipment, weather, food-safety, image-right, and identity risks.

Use `scripts/net_income.py` for arithmetic after sourcing each input.

## Channel Ladder

Prioritize channels based on verifiability, not popularity.

### Strong Starting Points

- school career center, faculty, alumni, or verified campus program;
- government public-employment services;
- employer website, official account, official recruitment system, or official switchboard;
- direct contact with the actual work location;
- a known employee whose identity and current relationship can be independently confirmed.

### Useful Leads Requiring Verification

- Xiaohongshu or other social posts from apparent employees;
- established recruitment platforms;
- student groups and alumni groups;
- intermediaries and labor-service companies;
- employee or student referral programs.

Treat each as a lead. Move the conversation toward verifiable company identity, role authorization, written terms, and independently obtained contact details.

### High-Risk Acquisition Patterns

- short-video direct messages, anonymous group chats, or personal payment accounts;
- mass recruitment for security, subway screening, factories, logistics, or “easy high pay” without a named actual work user;
- guaranteed placement after fees, certificates, training, deposits, or recruiting friends;
- remote interviews that require screen sharing, facial recognition, bank operations, or account creation.

High-risk does not mean automatically fraudulent. It means the student should not spend money, travel, or disclose sensitive data before resolving the missing facts.

## Gig Boundaries

Classify the actual task, not the attractive label.

Potentially assessable examples include legitimate event support, exhibition staffing, film extras, sports-event support, store promotion, tutoring, and personal skills such as braiding. Verify organizer identity, venue, hours, transport, image rights, insurance or injury handling, cancellation, and payment.

Require extra scrutiny for food sales, late-night events, private-home services, remote locations, crowd filling, or tasks collecting identity data.

Reject tasks involving:

- fake reviews, fake attendance, fake engagement, deceptive posting, or undisclosed advertising;
- opening or lending bank, phone, social, marketplace, payment, or company accounts;
- receiving or forwarding money or goods without a legitimate, documented purpose;
- illegal sales, evasion of permits, harassment, impersonation, or misuse of personal data.

## Referral Rewards

Do not encourage a new student worker to present themselves as an agent before verifying the role. Referring classmates can multiply harm and may change the student's responsibility.

Only explore a referral reward when:

1. the student has personally completed verification and normally joined;
2. actual conditions match written terms;
3. the employer confirms the referral program in writing;
4. the reward comes from the employer and is not deducted from recruits;
5. the student discloses their reward to classmates;
6. the student makes no guarantee and does not collect identity documents or money;
7. classmates independently receive and review the same terms.

Suggested wording:

> 我还有几位同学可能对这个岗位感兴趣。如果我本人正常入职并确认实际条件一致，公司是否有公开的员工推荐奖励？请书面说明支付主体、金额、发放条件，以及该奖励是否会从被推荐人的工资或费用中扣除。

## Outreach Scripts

### Direct Call To A Work Location

> 你好，我是大学生，计划在［日期］到［日期］做暑期工作。我从［渠道］看到贵店/单位可能招聘［岗位］，想直接核实一下：这个地点现在是否确实招聘短期学生？由哪家公司签约和发工资？入职需要哪些证件或培训？工资、工时、发薪日和食宿条件能否书面确认？

### Reply To A Social Lead

> 谢谢你的信息。我会通过公司官方渠道核实岗位。方便提供公开招聘链接、部门或门店名称、岗位要求和官方联系方式吗？请不要发送你的内部资料或个人证件。

### Request Comparable Terms

> 为了比较实际收入，请确认工资口径、预计可工作天数、每日工时、所有扣款、交通食宿成本、培训或等待时间，以及最晚发薪日期。
