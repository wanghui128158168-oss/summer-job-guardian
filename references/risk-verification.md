## Six-Party Map

Identify each party separately:

| Role | Question |
|---|---|
| Publisher | Who posted or forwarded the listing? |
| Interviewer | Who is selecting or onboarding the student? |
| Payee | Who receives any fee, deposit, rent, or other payment? |
| Contracting party | Which legal entity signs the agreement? |
| Actual work user | Who controls the site, schedule, and daily work? |
| Wage payer | Who calculates and transfers wages? |

For each party, record legal name, relationship, evidence, independently obtained contact, and unresolved mismatch. Personal payment accounts or unexplained layers increase risk but do not alone prove fraud.

## Five-Consistency Check

Compare the advertisement, chat confirmation, contract, arrival instructions, and actual work on:

1. role and duties;
2. city and exact work site;
3. wage basis, deductions, pay date, and overtime;
4. start date, duration, hours, rest, training, and waiting time;
5. certificate, medical exam, clothing, food, housing, transport, and other prerequisites.

Also compare the six parties across stages. Use `scripts/compare_terms.py` when the information can be normalized.

## Verification Ladder

Do not stop at the first successful check.

1. Confirm the exact legal entity and current public registration.
2. Check any relevant human-resources-service or labor-dispatch status through current official channels.
3. Confirm the named person is connected to that entity without using a phone number supplied only by that person.
4. Ask the actual work user whether the role, location, dates, headcount, intermediary, and prerequisites are current.
5. Obtain material terms in writing from the party responsible for them.
6. Compare payment recipient, contract seal, office signage, and written authorization.

A screenshot of a license or authorization can be altered, expired, generic, or unrelated. Verify identifiers through an independent source.

## Fees And Certificates

List every direct and indirect cost:

- medical examination;
- security, screening, occupational, or other certificates;
- training, course materials, insurance, files, background check, or onboarding;
- uniform, bedding, equipment, access card, or deposit;
- transport, pickup, housing, utilities, food, or management;
- platform, membership, referral, or placement fees;
- penalties, wage withholding, or refund conditions.

For each cost, ask who charges it, legal name of payee, amount, due date, basis, invoice or receipt, refund conditions, whether the actual employer requires it, and whether an independently chosen provider is allowed.

Treat a newly disclosed certificate or unpaid training period as a material change. Verify the certificate with the relevant authority or actual employer, not only the recruiter or training provider.

Conservative decision rule: before the role, work site, start date, prerequisites, contract party, and wage payer are independently confirmed in writing, advise against paying or traveling.

## Intermediaries

Do not label all intermediaries unreliable. Verify:

- exact legal identity and current contact information;
- current registration and any applicable license or permit;
- relationship with the actual work user;
- authorization for the specific role, site, date, and headcount;
- who employs, dispatches, manages, and pays the student;
- all fees and whether the student can verify the role before payment;
- process if no role is available, including refund timing and responsible entity;
- whether the intermediary can unilaterally substitute roles or cities.

“We have worked with them before” is not current authorization.

## Social Leads

Xiaohongshu and similar platforms can connect students to genuine employees, but intermediaries can imitate employees.

Use account history, continuity, and role-specific detail only as weak supporting signals. Do not ask for confidential internal material or personal identity documents. Move verification to:

- a public recruitment link;
- the employer's official system or switchboard;
- the specific store or site through independently obtained contact details;
- a written referral program or official email when available.

Refusal to move beyond a personal account, urgency, payment requests, copied content, and inconsistent employer details are risk signals. A polished long-term account is still not proof.

## Work-Site Observations

Record facts without judging appearance:

- exact address and whether it matches public and contract records;
- full company name on the door, rules, forms, badges, receipts, and seals;
- whether names are missing, covered, crossed out, or inconsistent;
- whether staff can state the legal entity and their role;
- whether licenses or permits relevant to the activity can be produced or independently verified;
- whether many applicants are waiting without named roles, schedules, or work sites;
- last-minute movement to another building, city, vehicle, hotel, or private residence;
- pressure to surrender documents, pay, sign blanks, or stop contacting others;
- emergency exits, public access, transport, and ability to leave.

An untidy office is not proof. Deliberate identity concealment, document mismatch, blocked verification, and unsafe control are decision-relevant.

## Hard Stops

Recommend `stop now` and give safety actions when applicable:

- immediate danger, threats, confinement, confiscation, or blocked exit;
- requests to open, lend, sell, or operate bank, phone, social, marketplace, or payment accounts;
- loans, screen sharing, remote-control apps, facial recognition, or transfers unrelated to verified work;
- unexplained travel or relocation after arrival;
- blank documents, concealed contracting identity, or refusal to name the wage payer;
- new fees, unpaid periods, certificates, or substituted roles combined with pressure to continue;
- recruiting others as a condition for refund, placement, wage, or commission;
- clearly deceptive, unlawful, or harmful task content.

An upfront fee without verified role details is a strong stop signal even when framed as small, refundable, standard, or urgent.

## Decision Language

- **Stop now**: a hard stop or unacceptable unresolved safety/financial risk exists. State what to stop and what safe action to take.
- **Pause and verify**: material facts are missing or conflicting. List the exact evidence needed and do not imply safety.
- **Continue cautiously**: material facts are sufficiently corroborated for the next limited step. State the scope and remaining risks. This is not a guarantee.
